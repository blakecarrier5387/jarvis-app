Privacy policy

Jarvis (personal) · last updated September 30, 2026

What Jarvis accesses

If you choose to connect Google, Jarvis asks for read-only access to your Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Drive and Google Contacts. It cannot send, change or delete anything in your Google account.

How it is used

Jarvis uses that access only on your own Mac, to answer the questions you ask it (for example, “what's on my calendar today?”). It is not used for advertising, profiling, or anything else.

Where your data goes

  • Your Google sign-in and any Google data Jarvis reads stay on your Mac. They are never sent to the developer of Jarvis or to any server the developer runs.
  • To answer a request, Jarvis sends it (and the information needed to answer it) to Anthropic's Claude using your own Claude subscription, under Anthropic's terms and privacy policy.
  • If you connect optional services (such as Telegram or ElevenLabs), messages you send through them go to those services under their own terms.
  • Jarvis does not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone else.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

You can revoke Jarvis's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, or remove Jarvis from its menu-bar icon → Uninstall Jarvis…, which deletes its files from your Mac.
